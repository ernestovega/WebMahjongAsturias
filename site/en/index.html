What is mahjong

Mahjong is a tile game for four players. Each player tries to build a winning hand of 14 tiles. You play it with strategy, memory and a little luck.

Mahjong is also a mind sport. It is one of the six games that the International Mind Sports Association (opens in a new tab) recognizes, together with chess, bridge, draughts, go and xiangqi. Read more.

Two ways to play

MCR

Chinese competition rules. Each hand scores points for its patterns. It is the main variant of the Spanish Federation.

Riichi

Japanese rules. It is the style of video games and anime. It has a fast pace and a lot of defense.

We know both variants. We teach the one you prefer. See the comparison in Learn.

Spanish Mahjong League

We play the Spanish Mahjong League (opens in a new tab) from Asturias. The league uses the online platform MahjongSoft (opens in a new tab). It also accepts games played in person. Read more in Federation and clubs.
