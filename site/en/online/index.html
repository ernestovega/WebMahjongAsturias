Play online

You can play Mahjong online for free from home. In fact, it is the best way to practise. These are the most used platforms.

The Spanish League

The Spanish Mahjong League (opens in a new tab) is played on MahjongSoft (opens in a new tab) for MCR and on Mahjong Soul (opens in a new tab) for Riichi. Any other platform with European rules is also accepted, as long as all four players are Spanish.

How to take part

  • Contact the Federation and fill in the league registration form (opens in a new tab).
  • On MahjongSoft, the Federation gives you a league tag.
  • On Mahjong Soul, the Federation gives you the league ID so you can join.

WhatsApp group

The Federation has a WhatsApp group where we organise and discuss games. You join by invitation. If you are interested, write to us at mahjongasturias@gmail.com or to the Federation at mahjongspain@gmail.com.
