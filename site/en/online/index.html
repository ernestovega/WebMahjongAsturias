Play online

You can play mahjong online for free from home. These are the most used platforms.

MahjongSoft and the Spanish League

The Spanish Mahjong League (opens in a new tab) is played on MahjongSoft (opens in a new tab). We play our league games there. MahjongSoft supports MCR and Riichi (RCR). It also has games against bots, an MCR trainer and a hand calculator. It also includes rules for the Duplicate format.

The League is also played on Mahjong Soul (opens in a new tab) (Riichi). Its website also lists Mahjong Time, Riichi City and Tenhou. To join, fill in the registration form (opens in a new tab).

Platforms

  • MahjongSoftMCRRiichiSpanish LeaguePlaying the Spanish League, practicing with bots and training. (opens in a new tab)
  • Mahjong SoulRiichiSpanish LeaguePlaying the Spanish League in Riichi. It has a tutorial and a clear interface. It is the best to start with Riichi. (opens in a new tab)
  • Mahjong TimeMCRRiichiand othersPlaying with people from all over the world. It has tournaments and leagues. (opens in a new tab)
  • Riichi CityRiichiPlaying Riichi games with players from many countries. (opens in a new tab)
  • TenhouRiichiPlaying against very strong players. The interface is simple and fast. (opens in a new tab)
  • BotzoneMCRPracticing against artificial intelligence programs. (opens in a new tab)
