Riichi yaku, fu and han

Download as PDF (opens in a new tab)

Blue: concealed hand only. Orange: loses 1 han if the hand is open.

1 han

  • Riichi Riichi (Concealed hand only)
  • Ippatsu Ippatsu (Concealed hand only)
  • Fully concealed hand Menzen tsumo (Concealed hand only)
  • Pinfu Pinfu (Concealed hand only)
  • Pure double sequence Iipeikō (Concealed hand only)
  • All simples Tan’yao
  • Dragon triplet Yakuhai
  • Seat wind triplet Jikaze
  • Round wind triplet Bakaze
  • After a kong Rinshan kaihō
  • Robbing a kong Chankan
  • Under the sea Haitei
  • Under the river Hōtei

2 han

  • Double riichi Daburu riichi (Concealed hand only)
  • Seven pairs Chiitoitsu (Concealed hand only)
  • Mixed triple sequence Sanshoku dōjun (Loses 1 han if the hand is open)
  • Pure straight Ittsū (Loses 1 han if the hand is open)
  • Half outside hand Chanta (Loses 1 han if the hand is open)
  • Triple triplet Sanshoku dōkō
  • Three concealed triplets San’ankō
  • Three quads Sankantsu
  • All triplets Toitoi
  • Little three dragons Shōsangen
  • All terminals and honours Honrōtō

3 han

  • Twice pure double sequence Ryanpeikō (Concealed hand only)
  • Half flush Hon’itsu (Loses 1 han if the hand is open)
  • Full outside hand Junchan (Loses 1 han if the hand is open)

5 han

  • Blessing of man Renhō (Concealed hand only)

6 han

  • Full flush Chin’itsu (Loses 1 han if the hand is open)

Yakuman

  • Thirteen orphans Kokushi musō (Concealed hand only)
  • Nine gates Chūren pōtō (Concealed hand only)
  • Blessing of heaven Tenhō
  • Blessing of earth Chihō
  • Four concealed triplets Sūankō (Concealed hand only)
  • Four quads Sūkantsu
  • All green Ryūiisō
  • All terminals Chinrōtō
  • All honours Tsūiisō
  • Big three dragons Daisangen
  • Little four winds Shōsūshii
  • Big four winds Daisūshii

Points by han and fu

Each cell: top, win by discard; bottom, win by self-draw (each opponent pays). 4 han 30 fu and 3 han 60 fu count as mangan. Each counter adds 300 points (100 per opponent on self-draw).
