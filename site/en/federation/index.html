Federation and clubs

We are an association affiliated with the Spanish Mahjong Federation (FEMJ (opens in a new tab)). Here are links to the federation and to the clubs in Spain.

How mahjong is organized

Organized mahjong has several levels. Each level belongs to the next one.

  1. Clubs and associations. They organize games, classes and local tournaments. Mahjong Asturias is at this level.
  2. Spanish Mahjong Federation (FEMJ) (opens in a new tab). It brings together the clubs in Spain. It is the Spanish member of the EMA (opens in a new tab).
  3. European Mahjong Association (EMA) (opens in a new tab). It brings together the European federations. It certifies tournaments and keeps the European ranking.
  4. World Mahjong Organization (WMO) (opens in a new tab). It brings together mahjong organizations from all over the world. The EMA (opens in a new tab) is affiliated with it. It promotes the MCR rules worldwide.

Separately, the Mahjong International League (MIL) (opens in a new tab) works on mahjong as a mind sport. Read more in Mahjong as a mind sport.

Spanish Mahjong Federation

The FEMJ (opens in a new tab) brings together the associations in Spain that play with the rules of the European Mahjong Association (EMA (opens in a new tab)). It organizes the official tournaments and runs the Spanish League.

Spanish Mahjong League

The league has a ranking for MCR and for Riichi. It is played on MahjongSoft (opens in a new tab) (MCR and Riichi), on Mahjong Soul (opens in a new tab) (Riichi) and also in person.

  • Ranking and results (opens in a new tab)
  • How to join (opens in a new tab). It asks for your name, your region, your EMA (opens in a new tab) number and your online usernames.
  • MCR uses the Green Book (2006). Riichi uses the EMA (opens in a new tab) 2025 rules.

EMA and WMO

European Mahjong Association (EMA)

The EMA (opens in a new tab) was founded in 2005. It certifies tournaments and organizes the European championships. The FEMJ (opens in a new tab) regulates the tournaments in Spain that count for the European ranking.

World Mahjong Organization (WMO)

The WMO (opens in a new tab) was created in 2005. It brings together mahjong organizations from all over the world. It promotes the MCR rules, which China set in 1998.

  • WMO website (opens in a new tab)

Mahjong as a mind sport

Mahjong is one of the six games that the International Mind Sports Association (IMSA (opens in a new tab)) recognizes as a mind sport. The other five are chess, bridge, draughts, go and xiangqi (Chinese chess). The IMSA (opens in a new tab) approved mahjong in April 2017.

The Mahjong International League (MIL (opens in a new tab)) represents mahjong in the IMSA (opens in a new tab). It was created in 2015 and has its headquarters in Lausanne, Switzerland. It brings together associations from more than 50 countries and regions. It recognizes three rulebooks: MCR, Riichi and Sichuan. Its goal is to bring mahjong closer to Olympic recognition.

The MIL (opens in a new tab) promotes the Duplicate format, in which all the tables play the same deals. See how it works, with a video, in Learn: Duplicate format.
